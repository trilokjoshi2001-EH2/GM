# Automechnics

## Register

Brand marketing website.

## Platform

Web, responsive desktop and mobile.

## Purpose

Help car owners understand eight service categories and prepare a service estimate enquiry.

## Audience

Car owners seeking routine maintenance, repairs and care. Service geography is pending.

## Brand

Precise, hands-on, reassuring. Central idea: Care you can see. User delegated creative direction and authorized implementation.

## Scope

Homepage, eight service detail pages, service overview, workshop story and enquiry preparation. No invented prices, warranties, reviews or business address.

## Content boundaries

Generated workshop imagery is illustrative and labelled. Enquiry delivery requires real business contact details; preview must not claim submission.

## Accessibility

Keyboard operation, readable contrast, visible focus, reduced motion and touch friendly controls..

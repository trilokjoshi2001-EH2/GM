# Automechnics design system
## Direction
Workshop precision expressed through strong condensed typography, real-world automotive imagery, directional arrows and spacious service information. Avoid repeated decorative cards and false social proof.
## Colours
White background; graphite ink; deep emerald primary anchored at hue 160; pale green surface; bright pale lime accent. Tokens use OKLCH.
## Typography
Barlow Condensed for display; Manrope for interface and body. Physical references: workshop signage and carefully printed service documentation.
## Layout
Framed desktop hero with asymmetric image and copy, eight concise service links, interactive vehicle overview and service-specific compositions. Mobile stacks naturally.
## Motion
One short entrance sequence, service hotspot transitions and purposeful hover changes. Native scrolling. Content visible by default. Reduced motion disables transitions.
## Assets
Generated primary logo and workshop photograph. Workshop photograph is illustrative until replaced by actual business photography.
